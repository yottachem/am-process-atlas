I used industrial FDM in an engineering group at a large instrument company, before today's prosumer printers existed. It saw limited use because it was so complex to run.

I can't say the heated chamber made much difference for what we printed, but I would expect engineering polymers like PEEK to be much better tolerated in that environment.

Are qualified materials worth the premium? Not in today's market, in my view.

Soluble supports are priceless. Many systems now get a similar result by printing supports in a dissimilar polymer, so the advantage is narrower than it was, but it is still generally useful.
