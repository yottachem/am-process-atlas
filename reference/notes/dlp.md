DLP gave me generally higher quality than masked SLA and better dimensional accuracy across many runs. I never had to deal with maintenance on the machines, though I was not a high-volume user.

The choice comes down to stage. In production, a qualified system is the better buy: you can point to its QA system instead of requalifying a new resin every time a material, environment, or other variable changes. You also save on operating costs, minimize maintenance, and get a little more traceability than masked SLA offers.

For prototyping, none of that matters much. If, like most users, you are iterating on designs, save your money: masked SLA and whatever resins you want will do the job.
