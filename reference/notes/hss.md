I have not run HSS or SAF, so this is an outside view. It is not clear to me whether SLS, MJF, and HSS/SAF are one category or three for a buyer. HSS prints an infrared absorber and sinters with a lamp, much like MJF [1], and all three work with the same few polyamides [2].

The differences seem to sit in thermal management, absorber chemistry, and build-area economics more than in the parts. A buyer comparing them should ask for sample parts and cost per part at their own volume, not a process name.
