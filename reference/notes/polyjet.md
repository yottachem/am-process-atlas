I have not run material jetting myself, so this is an outside view. Jetting systems offer more material capability than most resin printers, with mixed hardness and full color in one part, but only to a point. In my view they do not add much to the commodity prototyping markets that other processes already serve well, and the cost is very high.

Where jetting earns its price is the list on this page: multi-material and full-color appearance models, soft-touch look-alikes, and anatomical models. For functional prototypes, in-house resin or FFF is usually the better spend.
