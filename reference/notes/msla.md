Masked SLA is my first choice for high-resolution parts with demanding fine features. Between laser SLA, DLP, and MSLA, my choice usually came down to which printer was available; beyond print speed, I never saw a large difference.

Brittleness is the expected complaint. The bigger surprise is hardware cost: low-cost resin printers tend to have much shorter lifetimes than most FDM machines or commercial SLA systems, so budget for replacement.

Post-cure sets the properties. I have never had a problem from overcure and always have one from undercure. My practice is an ultra-clean IPA bath and an overly long cure, watching for overheating.

Two things caught me as a beginner: room temperature matters more than expected, and cleanup takes far longer than it should.
