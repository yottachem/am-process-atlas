I have not run SLS myself, so this is a materials view. The polymer list is short because the process demands very narrow, prescriptive thermal properties. The powder bed sits just below the melting point, and the polymer has to stay molten long enough to fuse without crystallizing and curling; the gap between melting and crystallization is called the sintering window [1]. Polyamide 12 has a wide one, which is why it remains the workhorse [1, 2].

Unsintered powder also ages in the bed, so the refresh ratio is part of the material, not just the process [2].
