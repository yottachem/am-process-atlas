I have not run LPBF, so this is an outside view. Most of the time, printed metal is the wrong answer. It is generally an extravagance unless the application is niche, such as rocketry, where one printed part can combine functions that would otherwise take an assembly [1].

Part of the cost is hidden. Dense parts come from a narrow window between lack-of-fusion voids at low energy and keyhole porosity at high energy [2], and most parts still need heat treatment and machining after printing.
